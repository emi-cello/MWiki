**Public World**
- New map: Cops and Robbers
  - A casual team arena for wild west chaos
  - Become a robber to steal loot from the bank vault
  - Become a cop to send robbers to jail and uphold the law
  - Become a civilian to stay neutral or cause chaos
- New map: Outlaw Oasis
  - A fast-paced PvP map based on community suggestions!
  - One player is chosen as the “outlaw” with extra damage and extra health
  - Everyone else is a bounty hunter tasked with eliminating the outlaw before time runs out
  - The outlaw can’t use health packs and can’t see the hunters, so the hunters need to use stealth, teamwork, and determination to win!
- New map: Mineshaft
  - Hop on these high speed rails to easily get from map to map in this vast desert
- New Map: Desert Town
