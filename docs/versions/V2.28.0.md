- Anniversary Hub will be available until next update!
  - This also acts as an easier way to access a few extra minigames
  - You can also still get your exclusive anniversary rewards

**Community Worlds**
- Renamed "Featured" worlds to "Trending" to improve clarity
- Community Worlds return you to Community World Central instead of Home
- Added easy access portals to both Popular and Trending worlds
- Added portals to your Recently Visited Worlds
- Added Hot Worlds
  - These portals show you random worlds that people are playing
  - Unlike popular/trending, these will be different every time you join a new lobby
- Updated Popular/Trending algorithm
  - Instead of being based on visit count, these are based on playtime (see #👀・trass-workshop for some of the nuances/protections)
- Community World fuel is now the same regardless of how many rooms you have
- Staff can join worlds that are out of fuel to refuel them (not just the admin)
- Trending Worlds can reappear more frequently (instead of waiting for 2 weeks)
- Fixed Random Worlds list to not show unfueled/closed worlds

**Changes**
- Disc Launcher deals less damage
- Beam Blades cost more stuffing to use
- Eaten grenades have reduced damage (but maintain the same range)
- Sharks don't like the taste of Sea Dragons
- Private World rooms cost 1000

**Fixes**
- Reduced the giant lag spike when opening imagination (noticeable when switching teams, switching rooms, or entering Dressing Room)
- Fixed text becoming invisible on Trading Cards and the Trading Card Binder when held with an outline
- Portal gun portals & in-flight portal gun projectiles now properly clear for everyone on a forced teleport (death teleport or team teleport)
- Fixed round timers being slow by 30 seconds for players joining an in-progress game in many game modes
- Fixed weird behavior when ghosts try to eat Anniversary Cake
- Fixed paint imagination category not updating properly when hiding/favoriting items
