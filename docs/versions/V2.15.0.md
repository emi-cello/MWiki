# Version 2.15.0


# v2.15.0 - Fishing Update
==========================
**Features**
- Added fishing system
- Added 3 Fishing Rods: Toy, Wooden, and Pro
  - More advanced fishing rods are more likely to catch large and rare fish
  - More advanced rods reel fish in faster
- Added fishing tension system
  - Reel too fast and your line will break, losing your catch
  - Watch the tension meter above your bobber
- Added 109 types of fish
  - This includes a unique species per biome!
- Added || fish slapping ||
- Fish can be deposited for Loot Quotas
  - You can earn butt-coins if fish are one of the weekly quotas
  - You can earn Silver Beans by trading in extra fish
- Added Bait Trader Block
- Added 4 types of fish bait
  - Each type of bait changes your probability of catching different things
- Added wacky junk items for you to catch
- Added Message in a Bottle with secret messages
- Added 6 charms that you can only get by fishing

- Added Fish Bucket
  - Collect fish that you've caught (or that others give to you)
  - Fish in your bucket will save between sessions
  - Take fish out of your bucket wherever you want
- Added the Legendary Fish Crown (3 colors)
  - Trade a complete fish collection (all non-legendary fish) and one Legendary Sea Dragon from your bucket in exchange for the crown
- Added Stuffed and Wooden Fish Catalog
  - Tracks your all-time catch history between sessions
- Added Fish Plaque

- Added Item Customization Earning Console

- Added RC Boat
- Added 4 new cooking recipes

- Added 2 Item Display pedestals
- Added Wall Mounted Item Display
- Added Banner Display
- Added Item Spawner

- Added Turtle Pet

- Added Fisherman Bundle

**Public World**
- New map: Fishy Harbor (5 new stashes)
- New-ish map: Retro Facility (5 new-ish stashes)
- Set up fishing spots in every biome
- Reorganized minigames area
- Added Charms Tech Web to Mega-Shop

**Changes**
- Updated colors for the Ocean Biome night sky
- Fabric Loot drops more often
- Visiting another Community World from a popular/featured Community World will not count towards the other world's "popularity"*
-# *This change will take a few days before the effects are noticeable

**Mobile**
- Fixed blocks appearing orange on Tech Webs

**Fixes**
- Chainsaw doesn't sound as weird in water
- Fixed weather particles going through the water
- Fixed no vote appearing when trying to kick players from a Community World
- Fixed sometimes not being able to add/remove Saved Outfits
