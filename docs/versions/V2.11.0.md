# v2.11.0 - Toymaker Update

**Features**
- Added item customization system
  - Recoloring
    - Change colors on most gadgets, including reskins
    - Color changes impact solid surfaces on gadgets (does not impact particle effects)
  - Charms
    - Place charms anywhere on the surface of a gadget as decorations
    - Charms can be placed on any static (non-animating) piece of a gadget
  - Saving
    - Save your customized item to access it any time
    - Customized items can be named
    - Customized items must be saved before they can be loaded into a dispenser or team bubble setter
  - Sharing 
    - Give your customized items to others and they can purchase it for themselves
    - Your name will be credited on all of your customized items
    - Disable sharing to prevent others from purchasing it
- Added Item Recolorer
- Added Charms Bench
  - Items on the Charms Bench take longer to despawn
  - Items on the Charms Bench can't be grabbed by other players
- Added 80+ charms
- Added Item Customization Tech Web
- Added Blank Banner
- Added customizable weather system
- Added 5 weather blocks (Fog, Rain, Thunderstorm, Snow, and Blizzard)
  - Weather blocks can be toggled with wiring for more control
  - Precipitation stops on ceilings
  - When you're in the rain, ||your umbrella will open!||

- Added Toymaker Santa Bundle
- Added Krampus Bundle

- Added Toymaker Giveaway
  - Enter in-game at the Toymaker Workshop
  - **Butt-coin prize**: 2,000 players each get 2,000 butt-coins
  - **Epic prize**: 5 players each get to design their own personalized cosmetic
  - **GRAND PRIZE**: receives above + gets to design their own reskin
    - The reskin can be of any gadget or pillow, and gets custom SFX and particle effects if they wish
  - More info here: https://discord.com/channels/1197017602292207666/1197585118148165722/1446989819069861928


**Imagination Revamp**
- Added new pages for Stuffed, Creative, Paints, and Favorites
- Reorganized Imagination to condense unnecessary categories, and break up overused categories
- Added Imagination Edit Mode
  - Long hold a bubble to enter Edit Mode
  - Add items to favorites for easy access
  - Hide items to clean up clutter
- Hovering over items in the Imagination for long enough reveals additional details
  - Items and categories show names
  - Pets show names and levels
  - SFX tapes and records play an audio preview
  - Records show songwriter names
- Pick Block automatically scrolls your Imagination to where the block is
- Bubbles get darker when they are restricted
- Mob spawners are now at the top of the "Mobs" category
- Settings appear in front of your imagination
- Team Bubble Setters prioritize your "favorite" reskin (or first unhidden reskin)

**Public World**
- New map: Toymaker Workshop
- Redecorated Home, beginner minigames, and the Adventure world
- Added hidden present stashes to Adventure world
- Added ||biome-exclusive charms||

**Changes**
- Team Bubble Setters now support Key Cards and local keys (e.g. Diamond Key)
- Team Bubble Setters work in Creative Mode
- Added City Gnome and Construction Gnome to Props Tech Web
- Added Letter, Hand Bell, and Bell Note Block to Music Tech Web

- Snowballs fly straight when launched from a bazooka
