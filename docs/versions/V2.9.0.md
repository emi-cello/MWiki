**Features**
- Added Pet Battles
  - Battle with up to 8 pets at once
  - Combat is turn-based
  - Command your pets to attack, defend, or charge
  - Pet levels affect battle outcomes
  - Each pet species is strong against some species, and weak to others
  - Winning a pet battle counts as a pet task (up to 3 total tasks a day)
  - Added ||pet battle music?||
- Added Pet Arena Block
- Added Stuffed Pet Arena Block
- Added pet backflips
- Added Pet Backflip Sign
- Added Dragon Bone props
- Added Dragon Statue
- Added Dragon Nest
- Added 5 new pet cosmetics
- Added setting to hide pet tasks

- Thanksgiving cosmetics are back for 2 weeks!

**Changes**
- ||Yeeps Plush|| can now ride on pets
- Added 4 new slots to the Pet Item Store
- You can now earn more coins by completing pet tasks
  - Increased the daily total pet task coin limit from 100 to 150
  - Pets almost always give coins after a pet task (until you reach the limit)
  - Pets always give 30 coins per task
- When a pet is hit with an explosion/baseball bat, its worn item will fall off

**Fixes**
- Fixed a bug where visual copies of items showed up in the wrong spots if held by a pet, anchored to a crossbow, anchored to a block, or in other similar situations
- Fixed Pet Dracula cosmetic not appearing when looking at pet cosmetics in Dressing Room
